# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Young professionals relocating between cities who need furniture fast without buying or hauling it. They're evaluating RentBasket at a stressful, logistics-heavy moment (new city, new home, tight timeline) and are comparing it against buying cheap furniture outright or going without. Secondary surface: the same users return post-purchase for catalog browsing, checkout, order tracking, and account management — so the site spans a marketing front door into a full browse-and-transact app.

## Product Purpose

RentBasket lets people furnish a new home through rental instead of purchase — lower upfront cost, no move-out disposal hassle, and flexibility for a life stage where relocation is common. Success today is largely already achieved: the product is fully built, deployed, and has received consistently positive feedback on the experience itself.

## Positioning

Renting furniture is the considerate way to set up a home you might not stay in forever — comfort without commitment, without waste.

## Brand Personality

Comfort, care, conserve — the brand's own stated motto. Premium, warm, and editorial in tone (not SaaS-bootstrap, not big-box-retail), with a turtle mascot carried across marketing sections. References: Article.com, West Elm, Linear, Vercel.

## Anti-references

Nothing should read as "vibe-coded." This is the single most important standing note for this project: multiple independent people have told the founder the site "looks really nice, but looks vibe-coded because of certain texts, or selection of things." The visual bones are already well-regarded — the smell comes from specific, fixable tells (placeholder-shaped copy, generic AI-pattern component choices, inconsistent micro-details) rather than the overall design direction. Treat any of the skill's own "AI slop" tells (gradient text, tiny uppercase eyebrows, numbered section scaffolding, identical card grids, hero-metric templates) as a hard fail if found, and hunt for copy and micro-detail tells with equal weight to visual ones.

## Design Principles

- Eliminate vibe-coded tells wherever found — in copy, component choice, and micro-interaction polish — rather than adding new surface area. This project's default mode is refine-and-verify, not build-new.
- Comfort, care, conserve as the throughline: every screen should feel considerate, not extractive or generic.
- Premium-editorial over SaaS-generic: favor the restraint and craft of Article.com/West Elm/Linear/Vercel over dashboard or template conventions.
- Consistency across an already-large surface: homepage, catalog, checkout, account, and order flows must all read as one system, not stitched-together sessions.
- Preserve what's working: the underlying visual system has been positively received — polish and de-genericize, don't redesign from scratch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: color contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation.
